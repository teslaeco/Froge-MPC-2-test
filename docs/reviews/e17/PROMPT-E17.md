# E17 reconstruction instruction

Work on a separate copy of the latest verified FORGE model. Read the previous export report, case memory and reference image. Preserve the original file and record the new input/output hashes. Do not substitute an image-generation result for a model render.

Target: improve likeness and anatomical/material continuity of the **adult** split living/skeletal woman. Use image coordinates: living side on the viewer's left; skeletal side on the viewer's right. Preserve the preferred R14/E15 head dimensions unless a measured reference discrepancy justifies a local correction. Retain the eye requested inside the skeletal socket. Keep the garment covering the bust; refine its shape and cloth support from the reference, without replacing the woman's proportions with a generic exaggerated body.

Priority order:

1. Register reference and model camera using visible landmarks: eye corners, nose base, mouth corners, chin, shoulder endpoints, neckline and waist. Mark occluded landmarks as unknown. Save the registration and a baseline render. Do not use a collage overlay as a texture when the packed original reference is available.
2. Rebuild the hairline so roots grow from the scalp and cover the cap-like gap. Shape broad, asymmetric flowing waves first. Add secondary clumps and sparse finer strands with taper and controlled variation. Living side: natural chestnut/brown. Skeletal side: aged grey/silver as newly requested. Keep the grey change labelled as art direction rather than exact reference colour. Avoid hundreds of identical cylindrical ropes, exposed blunt roots, a centre trench or a larger apparent skull.
3. Refine the dental arch and smile. Give incisors, canines and posterior teeth distinct crown proportions with softened edges; seat them in a curved alveolar arch. Maintain mouth-corner continuity. Keep all teeth behind the correct lip/cheek envelope; prevent duplicate rows, rectangular blocks and a dark floating mouth strip. Check front and profile in neutral material before colour.
4. Correct orbital rim, maxilla, nasal cavity and cheek/mandibular transition locally. The skull is a connected structure, not a painted black half-face. Preserve smooth live-side nose and cheek contours. Do not enlarge both eyes or the cranium to create a false impression of detail.
5. Blend neck into the base of the jaw and shoulders. Model restrained clavicular/neck relief with anatomically connected forms. Refine torso and garment together, checking side profiles, breast volume under the dress, axilla and sleeve attachment. Do not make a fabric seam or a neck tendon into a detached tube.
6. Refine materials after the large shapes pass review. Separate skin colour variation from roughness and micro-normal detail. Keep skin pores subtle and scaled consistently; do not bake heavy directional shadows into new colour maps. Make ivory cloth and black cloth visibly different in weave and roughness while maintaining matching seam geometry. Keep export-compatible maps for FBX/GLB; a native Blender shader alone is not sufficient.
7. Add geometry only to features that need silhouette/curvature/relief resolution. Measure the final exported count. Produce an optimized preview separately if needed; do not inflate the count by global subdivision to claim quality.
8. Export and re-import the final artifact in an empty scene. Verify units, object availability, UVs, active texture images and bounding dimensions. Render front, both oblique sides, one profile and back, plus face/teeth/hairline close-ups and neutral-material face. Use the same camera, lights and exposure for baseline/candidate pairs. Reject any candidate with a visible critical regression.

Return actual changed features, checks performed, remaining defects and the locations of model files and renders. Save the case facts, successful operations and rejected regressions in versioned project memory. Call this evaluation and application improvement unless a real weight-training job exists with a reproducible configuration, data manifest, checkpoint and held-out results.
